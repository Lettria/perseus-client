# Report on statsThis report summarizes the key performance indicators, financial statistics, and operational metrics for **L’Oréal** based on the 2024 Annual Report data.

### 1. Financial Performance
L’Oréal maintains its position as the world's No. 1 beauty company with robust growth across all primary financial metrics.

| Metric | Value | Growth / Change |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Revenue** | €43.48 Billion | +5.1% (Comparable) |
| **Operating Income** | €8.69 Billion | +6.7% |
| **Operating Margin** | 20.0% | — |
| **Net Profit Per Share** | €12.66 | +4.8% |
| **Proposed Dividend** | €7.00 | +6.1% |
| **Taxes Paid** | €2.8 Billion | — |

### 2. Divisional Growth & Market Position
All four divisions reported positive growth, led by Dermatological Beauty.

*   **Beauté Dermatologique:** +9.8% growth rate.
*   **Produits Grand Public (Consumer Products):** +5.4% growth rate.
*   **Produits Professionnels:** +5.3% growth rate.
*   **Luxe:** +2.7% growth rate (Leader in fine perfumery).

### 4. Digital & E-commerce
E-commerce continues to be a significant driver of total sales.
*   **E-commerce Revenue:** €12.3 Billion.
*   **E-commerce Share:** 28.2% of total revenue.
*   **E-commerce Growth:** +7.8%.

### 5. Innovation, Research & Data
L’Oréal invests heavily in "Beauty Tech" and scientific advancement.
*   **Annual R&I Investment:** Over €1 Billion.
*   **Research Workforce:** 4,000 researchers.
*   **Intellectual Property:** 694 patents filed.
*   **Skills Revolution:** 578 specific skills identified (focusing on AI and Data).
*   **Digital Reach:** Over 7 billion products distributed.

**Environmental Impact:**

*   **Renewable Energy Usage:** 97%.
*   **Recycled Water:** 53%.
