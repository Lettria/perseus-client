# Report on money kpisThis report summarizes the key financial performance indicators (KPIs) for L’Oréal based on the 2024 Annual Report data.

### 1. Group Financial Overview
L’Oréal demonstrates strong financial health with total revenue exceeding **€43 billion** and a robust operating margin of **20%**.

| KPI | Value | Growth / Change |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Revenue** | €43.48 Billion | +5.1% (Comparable) |
| **Operating Income** | €8.69 Billion | +6.7% |
| **Operating Margin** | 20% | — |
| **Net Profit Per Share** | €12.66 | +4.8% |
| **Taxes Paid** | €2.8 Billion | — |

### 2. Shareholder Value & Dividends
The company continues to increase its payout to shareholders, reflecting a consistent upward trend in dividend distribution.

*   **Proposed Dividend:** €7.00 (a **+6.1%** increase).
*   **Dividend History:** 9th consecutive year of award/growth recognition.

### 3. Revenue Breakdown by Division
Growth is diversified across various market segments, with **Beauté Dermatologique** leading in percentage growth.

| Division | Growth Rate | Key Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Beauté Dermatologique** | +9.8% | Highest growth segment |
| **Produits Grand Public** | +5.4% | Includes brands like L'Oréal Paris, Garnier |

### 4. Geographic Revenue Distribution
L’Oréal maintains a "multipolar" model with revenue spread across five major global regions:

*   **Europe:** 33%
*   **North America:** 27%
*   **North Asia:** 24%
*   **SAPMENA – SSA:** 9%
*   **Latin America:** 8%

### 5. Digital & Innovation Investment (Future Growth)
A significant portion of capital is reinvested into R&I and digital transformation to ensure long-term competitiveness.

*   **eCommerce Revenue:** €12.3 Billion (**28.2%** of total revenue), growing at **+7.8%**.
*   **Annual Investment in R&I:** Over **€1 Billion**.
*   **Intangible Assets/Innovation:** 694 patents filed; 37 global brands in the portfolio.
*   **Strategic Funds:** **€70 Million** allocated to the *Fonds L’Oréal pour les Femmes*.

### 6. Strategic Acquisitions & Licensing
To bolster its market position and revenue streams, L’Oréal engaged in several high-profile financial moves, including acquisitions or licensing agreements with:
*   **Miu Miu**, **Jacquemus**, **Galderma**, **Dr.G**, and **Amouage**.
