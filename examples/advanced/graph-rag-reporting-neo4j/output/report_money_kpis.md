# Report on money kpis### **L’Oréal Financial Performance Report: 2024 Money KPIs**

This report summarizes the financial health and monetary Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for L’Oréal, based on the 2024 annual data.

---

### **1. Core Financial Results**
L’Oréal maintains its position as the world's No. 1 beauty company with strong growth in both revenue and profitability.

| KPI | Value | Growth/Margin |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Revenue** | 43.48 billion € | + 5.1% (Comparable) |
| **Operating Income** | 8.69 billion € | + 6.7% |
| **Operating Margin** | 20% | — |
| **Taxes Paid** | 2.8 billion € | — |

---

### **2. Shareholder Returns**
Profitability metrics for shareholders show steady upward momentum.

*   **Net Profit Per Share:** 12.66 € (+ 4.8% growth).
*   **Proposed Dividend:** 7.00 € per share.
*   **Dividend Growth:** + 6.1%.

---

### **3. E-commerce Performance**
Digital sales continue to be a significant driver of the total business volume, representing nearly a third of all revenue.

*   **E-commerce Revenue:** 12.3 billion € (28.2% of total revenue).
*   **E-commerce Growth:** + 7.8%.

---

### **4. Divisional and Geographic Revenue Breakdown**

#### **Performance by Division**

| Division | Growth Rate | Key Market Position |

Revenue is globally distributed, with Europe and North America remaining the largest contributors.
*   **Europe:** 33%

*   **North Asia:** 24%
*   **SAPMENA-SSA:** 9%
*   **Latin America:** 8%

---

### **5. Strategic Investments and Allocations**
L’Oréal reinvests significant capital into innovation and social impact.

*   **Research & Innovation (R&I):** Over 1 billion € invested annually (supporting 4,000 researchers and 694 patents filed).
*   **Social Impact Funding:** 70 million € allocated to the *Fonds L’Oréal pour les Femmes* (active since 2020).
*   **Inclusive Sourcing:** Engagement with over 750 suppliers through the *Achats Inclusifs* program.

---

### **6. Asset and Brand Portfolio**
The group's financial strength is supported by a portfolio of **37 brands** and recent strategic licensing or acquisitions including **Jacquemus, Dr.G, Amouage, Galderma, and Miu Miu**. 

Additionally, the company maintains high ESG ratings (**CDP: AAA**, **EcoVadis: Platinum**), which increasingly correlate with long-term financial stability and reduced investment risk.
