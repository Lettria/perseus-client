# Report on money kpisThis report summarizes the financial Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and money-related metrics for L’Oréal based on the 2024 Annual Report data.

### 1. Global Financial Performance
L’Oréal maintains its position as the world's No. 1 beauty company with robust top-line and bottom-line figures. The company reported a total revenue of **€43.48 billion**, supported by significant digital integration.

| Key Metric | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Total Revenue** | €43.48 Billion |
| **Operating Income** | €8.69 Billion |
| **Operating Margin** | 20% |
| **Taxes Paid** | €2.8 Billion |
| **Ecommerce Revenue** | €12.3 Billion (28.2% of total revenue) |

### 4. Strategic Financial Investments
L’Oréal allocates substantial capital toward innovation and social impact, viewing these as drivers for long-term economic stability.

*   **Research & Innovation (R&I):** Invests over **€1 billion annually**.
*   **L’Oréal Fund for Women:** **€70 million** allocated since 2020.
*   **Inclusive Sourcing:** Engages with over 750 suppliers.
*   **Skin Technology:** Significant investment in bioprinted skin models and AI (CreAItech, Beauty Genius) to drive future product premiumization.

### 5. Governance and Efficiency
*   **Executive Supervision:** The Finance department is directly supervised by the 19-member Executive Committee.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Deployment of automated fulfillment centers to optimize the supply chain and protect margins.
*   **Board Composition:** Maintains an independence rate of 50%, ensuring fiscal oversight and strategic alignment with shareholder interests.
